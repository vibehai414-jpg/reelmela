For organizers, concretely

Instead of 500 separate flop posts about your fest, you get one collab reel, one aftermovie, and a live wall of clips landing at the venue — made by your own audience.

Why not the others?

COJO / Mesh / PolyTake are wedding-only at $249/event — no viral posting, no payment split. Vyclone shut down with no money model. Instagram Collab caps at 5 accounts. ReelMela is public events + split + Meet.

Safety, plainly

Meet is 18+, opt-in, off by default — no IG ID, phone or live location before a mutual match, block & report anytime. Clips are deletable anytime, private events stay private, royalty-free music only, DPDP Act 2023 consent flow.
