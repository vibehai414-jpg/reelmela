# ReelMela — Go-To-Market: Launch in Harda First

## Principle: Don't launch in India. Launch in 1 college, 1 event.

## Phase 1: Validation (Week 1)
- Pick 1 event: Harda College Fest / Local Garba / Wedding
- Talk to organizer: Offer FREE Aftermovie + Promotion
- Pitch: "I will get 50 students to post about your event, you get free marketing. I just need permission to put ReelMela banner."
- Get 30 students in WhatsApp group: "Join ReelMela, upload your clip, get free Aftermovie + chance to earn"

## Phase 2: First Event (Week 2)
- Day of event: QR codes at entry, 2 volunteers to help upload
- Live: Show clips on projector (Live Wall) — crowd loves seeing themselves
- Next morning: Post Collab Reel + Aftermovie, tag all 30
- Payout: Even if pool is small (₹1,000), pay 5 top creators ₹200 each, screenshot payout, post as proof

## Phase 3: Proof Content (Week 3)
- Make 3 reels: 1) "We made Aftermovie from 30 phones" 2) "We paid creators" 3) "Meet story: Two people met at same event via ReelMela"
- Post in Harda college groups, Indore/Bhopal groups
- Get 2 more organizers to sign up

## Phase 4: Expand (Month 2)
- Target: 5 events in Harda + Indore + Bhopal
- Hire 2 campus ambassadors per college (₹500 + PRO free)
- Partner with 1 local cafe per city as sponsor

## Marketing Channels (Free)
- WhatsApp Groups (college), Instagram Collab posts, College notice boards, QR posters
- No paid ads until 3 events done

## KPIs
- Week 2: 30 uploads, 1 aftermovie, 50k views
- Month 1: 3 events, 150 uploads, 10 PRO sales
- Month 3: 15 events, 1,000 users, ₹50k revenue
