3. **Sponsor / Brand**

4. **Meet PRO (Dating)**

   - Future: ₹299/month Meet+ (like Tinder Gold)

## B. Earnings Split Formula (Transparent)
Pool = Organizer Fee (50%) + Sponsor (50%) + Ad (future)
Example: Pool = ₹10,000, Total WatchTime = 10,000 sec
User A watchTime = 1,000 sec -> Earning = (1000/10000)*10000 = ₹1,000
PRO boost: PRO users get 1.5x multiplier, capped at 30% of pool.

Wallet shows: Your clip: 12 sec used, WatchTime: 1,000 sec, Your share: 10%, Earning: ₹1,000
