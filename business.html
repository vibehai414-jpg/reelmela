Don't hire one influencer. Flood your shop with many.

Normally you message one influencer, fix a price, wait for one post — and hope people see it. On ReelMela you do it differently: many local creators visit your shop on the same day, film there, and post at once. Your shop shows up again and again in local feeds — from different people, different angles, all on one day. That is what makes people in your own city notice and walk in.

It works for a new shop opening, a sale, a new menu, a festive offer — or any day you want your shop to be the place everyone is talking about.

Organisers: build the hype before the event

Don't wait for the event day to start promoting. Ask creators on ReelMela to post teaser clips, countdowns and behind-the-scenes reels in the days before your event — the stage going up, rehearsals, the venue getting ready. By the time the gates open, the crowd already knows, and the views are already building.

One budget. You pay for views, not just for posting.

Pick your shop or your event, set one budget, and hire many local creators at once — no separate DMs, no separate deals with each influencer. Creators then earn from the verified views their one payout post actually gets. So your money goes to reach that really happened, not just to a post that went up.

Your budget is split by verified views — a creator's verified views ÷ total verified views × your budget, capped at 30% per creator, ₹100 minimum payout.

Star creators

Creators with a big following — their one post can pull thousands of views on its own. Fewer creators, bigger splash.
